Empirical finding: The naive 5-day EWMA fails the zero-baseline kill criterion on every tested fund. Two structural reasons explain why: (1) roughly half of all trading days have zero net share creations (52.9% nonzero overall), and (2) equity ETFs like NOBL and REGL experience quarter-end ETF heartbeat trades (large in-kind creations immediately followed by matching redemptions for tax-basis management), causing EWMA to extrapolate spikes into next-day reversals (negative Pearson correlation on NOBL and REGL).
